Floppy

Federated Learning on a Peer to Peer system

Objective and Innovation

Our goal was to decentralize AI training across multiple devices, eliminating the need for data aggregation at a central point. To achieve this, we developed Floppy, an algorithm that leverages asynchronous Federated Learning and integrates it with Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC), thus preserving data privacy and enabling learning from decentralized data sources.

Protecting Data in Distributed Deep Neural Networks

In today's privacy-conscious world, safeguarding data shared with third parties is crucial. When sensitive information enters distributed Deep Neural Networks, there is a genuine risk of privacy violations.

Novel Approach - Floppy

Introducing Floppy, an innovative algorithm that implements asynchronous Federated Learning and Secure Multi-Party Computation (SMPC) to ensure privacy during collaborative training.

Resilient and Efficient

Floppy's asynchronous nature makes it resilient against slower nodes, ensuring a smooth training process while preserving data privacy.

Real-World Evaluation

We evaluate Floppy, along with other techniques, using iid and non-iid image datasets to analyze their impact on computer vision tasks, demonstrating its effectiveness in maintaining data privacy.
